name: adr
description: Propose or review a ScreamPad Architecture Decision Record when a consequential design choice arises.
---

Read existing decisions, `docs/architecture/decisions/template.md`, and `docs/engineering/development-workflow.md`. Compare credible options, costs, security, migration, and validation. Draft new records as Proposed. Obtain owner approval before implementation or changing any Accepted ADR; never self-approve a decision.
